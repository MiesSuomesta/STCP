STCP benchmark-only overlay v3

Scope:
  tests/benchmark/raspberrypi/benchmark_client.py

Includes v2:
  - bounded concurrent TX/RX pipeline
  - synchronized timed start after all clients connect
  - stop new sends at deadline and drain outstanding replies

Adds v3 diagnostics:
  --verbose
  --stats-interval SECONDS

Verbose fields:
  RUN/CONNECT/DRAIN phase
  interval TX MiB/s
  interval RX MiB/s
  interval ops/s
  total outstanding requests
  active clients
  clients currently draining
  errors

Example:
  python3 benchmark_client.py --mode tcp --host 192.168.1.20 --port 19000 \
    --clients 8 --payload 1048576 --pipeline 8 --duration 10 --verify \
    --verbose --stats-interval 1

No kernel/STCP core/carrier changes.
